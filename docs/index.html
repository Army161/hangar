Features

Everything your Task Manager won't tell you

Hangar goes beyond the process list. See who owns what, what's listening, and what you can safely shut down.

Owners View

Every process grouped by the real app or project that owns it, not the executable name. See your stack, not a flat list.

Port Wall

Every listening port, probed for HTTP, labelled with its page title, clickable. Know what's bound and who put it there.

Origins

Everything configured to start itself, with dated entries and a reversible off switch. Take back your boot sequence.

Graveyard Scanner

Dormant projects ranked by what cleaning them up would reclaim. Stop old projects from haunting your machine.

Safety Gates

Local-First

Safety Model

Three gates between you and "oops"

Every destructive action in Hangar passes through three safety gates. Nothing is killed without your explicit approval.

1. Dry Run

See exactly what would happen before anything changes. Hangar simulates the action and shows you the full impact.

2. Typed Confirmation

No accidental clicks. You type the action name to confirm. This ensures deliberate, conscious approval for every operation.

3. Re-evaluation

After every action, Hangar re-scans and shows you the new state. If something went wrong, restore it instantly.

Every action is reversible. Park processes instead of killing them. Disable startup items instead of deleting them. Always a way back.
